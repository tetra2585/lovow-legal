FAQ

Can I keep my records when I change phones?

Yes. Create an account with Apple or Google under Settings → Account, and your records are backed up automatically (free). Sign in the same way on your new phone to restore them.

How do I connect with my partner?

Go to Settings → Partner → Send an invite, and send it to your partner. They open the link, or enter the code under "Enter an invite code". Both of you need an account to connect.

Can I connect with more than one person?

One person on the free plan, up to five with Premium. Each connection has its own space for two; switch between them with the names at the top of Home. Records with one person are never shown to another. Premium is shared by the purchaser and the first person they connected with. When Premium ends, spaces from the second person on become read-only (nothing is deleted).

Can my partner see promises marked "Just me"?

No. Neither the content nor the fact that they exist is shown. For a Surprise, they only learn that something is planned for them until you deliver it.

How do I disconnect from my partner?

Go to Settings → Partner and tap Disconnect for that person. You'll no longer see what you shared with each other. What each of you wrote stays with its author.

How do I cancel Premium?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → LOVOW. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Your records and photos stay after you cancel.

My Premium purchase isn't showing

Tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose 日本語 or English.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that they're on under Settings → Notifications, and that you're not in quiet hours. Also check that notifications are allowed for LOVOW in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and all your records are deleted right away, and this can't be undone. Deleting doesn't cancel your subscription, so please cancel it first.
